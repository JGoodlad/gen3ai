# Adversarial review: X62, team recombination (2026-10-27)

**Scope.** [`design_x62_team_recombination.md`](../design_x62_team_recombination.md) (`fca58b24`, corrected
`5e865f3b`), read against what it cites: the piloting-gap forensics (`c758fcb8`), the H-MID review (`90708bff`), the
N2 identity test (`fd4160d7`), the context-generalisation survey (`5d554c23`), the strategy doc (`7574743d`), the
H-CTX PREREG with its Amendments 1 and 2, and the D1 / D3 / N1-P corpus builders (`utils.team_corpora`). The design
was not edited.

**Method.** All checks are CPU only, run at nice 19 on the agent cores with no GPU, and each takes under a minute.
The scripts are in
[`x62_design_2026-10-26/review_2026-10-27/`](../../research_state/measurements/x62_design_2026-10-26/review_2026-10-27/):
- `recomb_draw_sim.py` is a Python-only prototype of the proposed draw (§2 steps 1–3). It reuses rc72b's own
  `draw_team` with the pool's species counts as the profile. It does not run the Node validator: the One Boost Passer
  Clause and the Hidden Power class are emulated from the set text. It plays nothing and writes nothing. It has two
  logs, `recomb_draw_sim.log` and `recomb_draw_sim_no_rc72b.log`.
- `power_checks.py` holds the Monte Carlos (seeded) behind sections A–E below. Its log is `power_checks.log`.
- The design's `set_familiarity.py` was re-run and reproduces its committed log byte for byte.

Abbreviations follow the design: pp = percentage points; LB = lower bound; F = `forum72`; C0 = the plain-continuation
control; S = in-house strength on home72; P = S's co-primary contrast; NI = non-inferior; σ_s = the between-seed SD
of a fork's true F score; AM-5 = D1's √max(1, φ̂) widening of the interval for seed overdispersion (φ̂ = the measured
dispersion ratio); resource-day = (GPU-h + lane-h) / 24 (the strategy doc's unit); rc72b = H-CTX's live panel
(rc72 is RETIRED).

## Verdict

- **The corpus construction holds up, and better than the design claims.**
  - The ≤ 4 within-corpus cap fits 12,000 teams (its FINDING 3).
  - The species profile stays within 0.046 total variation of the pool's.
  - Every one of the pool's 658 sets appears in the corpus.
  - The legality analysis is right at the source.
- **X62 is distinct from D1 and N1-P, as the design says.**
- **The gaps are in the decision layer:**
  - The co-primary is set up to fail under the lever's own hypothesis (B1).
  - The tree never consults PR-1, the strategy's first branch, whose KILL forbids exactly this fork (B2).
  - Amendment 2 (rc72 → rc72b) never reached the doc (M1).
  - The headline odds are 1.5–2.5× too high on the doc's own stated assumptions (M2).
  - The NULL branch blames plasticity, which a later read measured as NO-LOSS (M3).
  - The H-CTX gate cannot separate SUPPORTED from UNDECIDED in the range its author predicts, yet the tree treats
    those two very differently (M4).
- **A GAIN would be interpretable** as "context breadth on familiar sets moves F". It would not, on the doc's own
  predictions, deliver goal (b) on U (m6).

## BLOCKER

**B1. The co-primary on in-pool strength is underpowered, and its prediction contradicts the hypothesis it is
conditioned on. Under H-CTX, "GAIN, P NON-INFERIOR" is close to unreachable.**

*The prediction.* The design predicts Δ_P −1.0 pp, with an 80 % range of (−2.5, +0.5). Its reason is that "X62 keeps
every set's exposure, so it is predicted to cost less" than D1's −1.61. That is the SET-LEVEL account's reasoning.

Every one of X62's predictions is conditioned on H-CTX SUPPORTED. Under H-CTX, the in-pool edge IS the memorised
pool sixes, and X62 halves their exposure exactly as D1 did: share 0.5 on both sides, ≈ 395 pilots per pool team
per 20M. Under its own premise, then, X62 should cost about what D1 cost (−1.6 pp), or more, because X62 trains
nothing that resembles a pool six.

*The power* (`power_checks.log` C). The rule is Welch 97.5 %, NON-INFERIOR iff LB > −2.0 pp, 5 seeds against 5. The
per-seed SDs come from D1's final: 0.86 pp for the arm and 0.35 pp for C0.

| true Δ_P | −1.61 (D1's cost) | −1.0 (the design's point) | −0.5 | 0 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P(NON-INFERIOR) | **0.07** | **0.36** | 0.68 | 0.89 |

Averaged over the design's own GUESS, P(NI) is **0.43**. Joint with GAIN (independence assumed; D below), the odds of
the design's success class are **0.21–0.24** (σ_s 1 to 0). If the true cost is D1's, they are ≈ 0.04. The modal positive outcome
is "GAIN, P not non-inferior", which the tree routes to the owner as a TRADE with **no exchange rate registered**.

*The literature.* The survey's recommendation 1(a) asked for exactly this to be fixed: "register its in-pool cost as
the expected price, not a harm bar". CoinRun's train score falls as levels rise, and so does Hill's train accuracy.

**Fix.**
- Re-derive Δ_P under H-CTX, about D1's −1.6.
- Replace the margin test with a pre-registered exchange rate: a GAIN on F of ≥ x pp is adopted at an in-pool cost
  up to y pp, with the owner's ruling written BEFORE the read.
- Alternatively, power P for the margin. The arm's seed SD dominates, so that means seeds, not pairs.
- State P(GAIN ∧ NI) beside P(GAIN) everywhere the odds are quoted.

**B2. The decision tree ignores PR-1, and the strategy doc forbids this fork on PR-1 KILL.** PR-1 is the strategy's
first branch. It pools D1, D3 and N1-P against C0 on F. Its KILL "retires breadth at the plateau, ≤ 20M, at these
corpora … no further plateau breadth fork" (strategy §3.1). X62 is a 20M plateau breadth fork.

The design's G1 registers X62 on N1-P NULL, LOSS or UNDECIDED-final, and calls N1-P NULL the branch where "X62 adds
the most information". N1-P NULL is also the most likely road to PR-1 KILL, since N1-P is a third of PR-1's estimand.
So the two always-current docs route the same likely world in opposite directions. The design itself says its
ranking rules are the strategy doc's. The strategy doc's ranking table has no X62 row.

**Fix.**
- Add PR-1's verdict as a node above G1:
  - **BREADTH-GAIN** ⇒ X62 is offered to the stack, as on N1-P GAIN;
  - **KILL** ⇒ X62 runs only under a written exception;
  - **UNDECIDED** ⇒ as now.
- The exception has to be argued, for example: "KILL tested set-novel corpora; X62 is the context-only form, and
  D1's recombined-like 31 % is the only context dose PR-1 saw". It needs the strategy owner's sign-off, and it has
  to be recorded in BOTH docs.
- Add an X62 row to the strategy's ranking table.

## MAJOR

**M1. Amendment 2 (rc72 → rc72b) never reached the design.**
- **Stale names.** G0 ("The H-CTX read (rc72, …)"), the §2 refusal list, M1 and §1.2 all name **rc72**. rc72 was
  RETIRED before any game counted. The read that routes this tree plays **rc72b** (PREREG Amendment 2, pin
  `905c29f5b`).
- **The refusal matters.** In the prototype, drawing without the rc72b refusal puts **491 of 12,000 corpus teams at
  ≥ 3 shared sets with ONE rc72b team (7 at 5)** (`recomb_draw_sim_no_rc72b.log`). That is inside the radius the
  design itself treats as a fit. With rc72b refused, every team is at ≤ 2 (`recomb_draw_sim.log`). A builder that
  follows the doc as written would contaminate M1, and any later rc72b read on X62's checkpoints.
- **The Hidden Power rule.** §2 step 4 cites "rc72's `hp_set_check.js` check". That is the `validated` rule, which
  Amendment 2 replaced for panels with `strict`. In the corpus, **17.2 % of slots (12,389 of 72,000) and 69 % of
  teams (8,334) carry a Hidden Power set with no IV line**, the class the live server rejects. Under `strict`, 69 % of
  candidates would be refused, and the redraws would shift set exposure away from Hidden Power carriers: a set-level
  change in a lever defined as "contexts only". `validated` matches what P\* trained on (the pool holds 739 such
  mons). But it means X62 trains on a set class that rc72b (its gate), F and home72 do not contain.

**Fix.**
- Name rc72b throughout.
- Refuse rc72b at radius 3.
- Choose `validated` explicitly for the training corpus, and say why.
- Carry Amendment 2's open question as a FINDING: does the no-IV class play its named type in training?

**M2. The headline odds and per-resource-day rate are overstated by 1.5–2.5× on the design's own assumptions.**

*What § 0 quotes.* "P(GAIN) 0.52 / 0.65" and "≈ 0.09–0.10 per resource-day". These rest on three choices:
- The **+2.0** GUESS. That GUESS is for "N1-P not GAIN", but X62 is registered only on NULL, LOSS or UNDECIDED-final,
  where the design's own GUESS is +1.5 (§4, §6).
- The PR rule's closed form **without AM-5**. With AM-5 as `main.x35.d1` computes it, power at +1.5 pp is **0.74 /
  0.66 / 0.46** at σ_s 0 / 0.5 / 1, against the doc's 0.80 / 0.67 / 0.44 (`power_checks.log` A; the strategy doc's
  own figure is 0.74).
- The **banked-C0** lane cost, which §8 item 9 itself says not to assume.

*Recomputed* (`power_checks.log` D) on the branch where X62 runs, by the PR rule with AM-5:

| σ_s | P(GAIN) | P(GAIN ∧ NI) | per resource-day, C0 banked | per resource-day, C0 fresh | joint, C0 fresh |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.56 | 0.24 | 0.067–0.092 | 0.046–0.065 | 0.020–0.028 |
| 1 pp | 0.49 | 0.21 | 0.058–0.080 | 0.040–0.056 | 0.017–0.024 |

The comparison with "the breadth stack (0.12) and H-TEAMS (0.09)" holds only on the strategy table's convention
(about 107 lane-h, P(GAIN) alone). That convention flatters every breadth row, which carries the same in-pool
co-primary. So the correction belongs in the strategy table too (FINDING F3).

**Fix.**
- Quote the run-branch GUESS, the AM-5 power and the fresh-C0 cost as the headline, with the banked-C0 figure as the
  conditional.
- Add P(GAIN ∧ NI).

**M3. The NULL branch blames plasticity, which has been measured as not lost. A NULL at 20M cannot retire context
breadth.**
- **What the tree says.** On NULL, G3 says "with N1-P NULL as well: plasticity is the leading explanation … Context
  breadth at the plateau is retired for this era", and §4's NULL row routes to N5-S.
- **What has been measured since.** The plasticity check (`8bebdfe3`, 01:29 on 10-27, after this draft at 00:04)
  read **NO-LOSS**: P\*'s refit AUC is 0.986× X26 @25M's. Later edits to the doc did not propagate it.
- **What the literature says.** The survey's recommendation 2 names dose as the live alternative. Every breadth
  result it cites trained on the broad distribution for 10⁸ steps, or, for the one specialist-first recipe (Honor of
  Kings), followed a specialist with a LONG broad continuation.
- **What X62 actually doses.** X62 gives the corpus ≈ 285k games a side, about 7 % of P\*'s 280M steps. The +2.0
  GUESS recovers 40 % of the addressable premium in that 7 % with no stated basis.
- **No dose read.** The K1 ⊂ K3 ⊂ ALL tiers "support a later dose read", but none is registered.

**Fix.**
- On NULL, route to the survey's long continuation on the union corpus (≥ 100M, pool ≤ 0.2) and the strategy's row 6
  (fork extension), not to plasticity.
- Strike "retired for this era".
- Register a reported dose line, for example F at the 10M checkpoint as well as at 20M, from the same seeds.

**M4. The H-CTX gate (G0) cannot resolve SUPPORTED from UNDECIDED where its author predicts the truth lies, yet the
tree treats the two very differently.** The registered rule decides on point estimates of R against 0.25 P and 0.75 P,
with 1,200 rc72b games and 800 each of home72 and F. Taking X51's 0.598 / 0.530 as the truth gives these verdict
probabilities (`power_checks.log` E):

| true R / P | INCONCL. | SUPPORTED | UNDECIDED | KILLED |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 (pure H-CTX) | 0.21 | **0.61** | 0.18 | 0.00 |
| 0.25 | 0.21 | 0.36 | 0.41 | 0.02 |
| 0.40 (PREREG author's upper) | 0.21 | **0.21** | 0.51 | 0.07 |
| 0.60 | 0.21 | 0.07 | 0.51 | **0.21** |
| 1 (set-level) | 0.21 | 0.00 | 0.17 | 0.61 |

At the predicted 0.25–0.40, whether X62 is REGISTERED (SUPPORTED) or run only as a D1-rule screen behind N1-P
(UNDECIDED) is close to a coin flip. A context share of 0.6 (most of the premium team-level) is SHELVED one time in
five. The gross misroutes are rare: SUPPORTED at R / P = 1, or KILLED at 0, happens < 0.01 of the time.

**Fix.** Do one of these:
- route SUPPORTED and UNDECIDED identically for X62, varying only the prior;
- or route by the bootstrap interval of R / P, not its point. For example, SHELVE only if R / P's LB is > 0.5.

## MINOR

- **m1. Set exposure is close to the pool's, not equal to it** (`recomb_draw_sim.log`).
  - Set-level total variation from the pool's occurrence profile is 0.073.
  - The corpus-to-pool exposure ratio of a set runs 0.81–1.56 from the 5th to the 95th percentile.
  - The ≤ 2 rule under-draws the core: Tyranitar's share of slots falls from 0.106 to 0.091 and Swampert's from 0.098
    to 0.081.
  - "A set is drawn as often as the pool repeats it" holds only approximately. Report both TVDs in the manifest, and
    say whether a reweight is wanted.
- **m2. Species-level distance is unconstrained.**
  - 279 of 12,000 prototype teams (2.3 %) have exactly a pool team's six species, and 44 % share ≥ 5 species with one
    pool team.
  - That is not closer than the test panels: F is at 68 % and H at 69 % (21 H teams have a pool six's species).
  - It is not a read hazard, but the "new six" claim is set-level only. Report it, and optionally refuse an exact
    species-six match.
- **m3. "False GAIN 0.025 with a correct widening" assumes σ_s is known.** With AM-5's estimated φ̂, false GAIN is
  0.018 / 0.028 / 0.040 at σ_s 0 / 0.5 / 1 (`power_checks.log` A).
- **m4. The shared control correlates the family's false GAINs.**
  - Conditioning on N1-P not GAIN barely moves X62's power: 0.465 → 0.428 at σ_s 1 with N1-P truly +1.5.
  - But X62's false GAIN given N1-P GAIN rises from 0.038 to **0.104** at σ_s 1 (`power_checks.log` B).
  - §8 item 7's 0.10–0.19 family-wise figure assumes independence. With one C0, false GAINs cluster, which matters
    most for "X62 GAIN and N1-P GAIN" readings.
- **m5. The in-house S read is missing from the cost table.** The co-primary re-plays C0 and plays every X62 final in
  S's regime (7 members × 500 mirrored pairs). D3 look 2's 5 finals cost ≈ 7.5 h of the same heavy slots, so 10
  finals cost ≈ 15 h (ESTIMATE).
- **m6. F is primary, so a GAIN does not mean goal (b).**
  - The design's own predictions give Δ_U ≈ (2.0 + 1.0 + 0.5) / 3 ≈ +1.2 pp.
  - X62 cannot reach H's or P72's unfamiliar sets (Packer's extrapolation, the survey §6.1).
  - State that a GAIN on F leaves goal (b) unmet, and register U's Δ beside F's as the goal-(b) number.
- **m7. The pool is not drawn uniformly on the trainee side.** X26's trainee draw is `default_biased` with
  `bias_prob` 0.1 toward the curated sample teams (`models/rb_x26_s2601/metadata.json`). At share 0.5 that becomes
  0.5 corpus / 0.45 pool / 0.05 sample. The pilots-per-pool-team arithmetic and the "usage-matched to what P\*
  trained on" profile ignore it. Whether the sample teams are home72 is UNVERIFIED here. If they are, the in-house S
  read on home72 loses exposure to its most-practised teams.
- **m8. The training corpora are not refused.** rc72b refused overlap with D1, D3 and N1-P; X62 does not. That is
  harmless for X62's read, but the survey's union corpus would hold near-duplicates. Report the overlap in the
  manifest.
- **m9. The nickname is not a line.** "The nickname line is dropped" means stripping the nickname from the head line
  (`Nick (Species) @ Item`) while keeping the species, gender and item. Specify the rewrite and test it.

## What holds (verified)

- **D1 and N1-P are not X62.**
  - `set_familiarity.log` reproduces byte for byte.
  - D1 is 624 / 1,981 recombined-like (≈ 16 % of draws at share 0.5, uniform within the tier, as `TeamMix` draws).
  - N1-P is 73 / 12,000.
  - D1's U +2.10 [−0.92, +5.11] and in-pool −1.61 [−2.90, −0.32] match the ledger (2026-10-24, D1 look 2).
- **The within-corpus cap fits** (the design's FINDING 3 resolves in its favour).
  - 12,000 of 12,000 indices are accepted, with 10 refusals under the cap.
  - The histogram of shared sets with an earlier accepted team is {0: 4, 1: 84, 2: 3,001, 3: 8,137, 4: 774}.
  - The Python draw takes ≈ 15 s. The Node validator's time is still unmeasured (FINDING 4 stands).
- **Legality at source** (`deps/pokemon-showdown`).
  - `[Gen 3] OU` = Standard + One Boost Passer Clause + Accuracy Trap Clause + Freeze Clause Mod + Speed Pass Clause.
    Gen 3's Standard has no Item Clause.
  - The team-level rules are Species, Nickname and One Boost Passer.
  - The pool holds 21 boost-passing mons, and no pool team has two. The prototype refused 4 candidates for the
    clause, so it is the rare refusal the design expects.
- **Separation from the held-out panels holds.** The team-mix startup guard's near-neighbour radius is 4
  (`NEAR_NEIGHBOUR_MIN_SHARED`), so a corpus refused at 3 passes it by construction.
- **Cost arithmetic.** The lane-h rows reproduce from their game counts at 160–230 games an hour. The GPU-h
  (≈ 4.1 a fork) matches N1-P's dose record.

## FINDINGS (standing rule 7)

1. **F1.** The prototype is not the builder. It skips the Node validator, the level pack, the engine scan and the
   label check, and it emulates the One Boost Passer Clause and the Hidden Power class from text. The real
   acceptance rates and build time are unmeasured.
2. **F2.** The power Monte Carlos model F as one binomial panel at base 0.49. They model seed noise as normal. They
   approximate N1-P's D1 rule as a single-look LB > 0, without the sign rule or looks. The co-primary's seed SDs come
   from one 5-seed final each. Every figure is ±0.01–0.02 of Monte Carlo error.
3. **F3.** The strategy doc's per-resource-day column ranks breadth rows by P(GAIN) alone, although each carries an
   in-pool co-primary with D1's −1.6 pp as precedent. B1's correction applies to the stack and H-TEAMS rows as well.
   That is a finding for the strategy doc, outside this review's scope.
4. **F4.** M4's table takes X51's cross-engine home72 and F as the truth. On rc72b's engine both may move, which
   moves INCONCLUSIVE's 0.21.
5. **F5.** This unit played no game, built no corpus, and changed nothing under `data/` or in the design.

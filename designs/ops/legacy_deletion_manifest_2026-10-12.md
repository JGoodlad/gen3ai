**Status: PROPOSED 2026-10-12, pending the owner's decisions in §0. A document only: nothing is deleted by it.**

follows `legacy_removal_manifest.md` and `deletion_pass_manifest.md` (decisions, rows, units, exit criterion,

**The direction.** The end-state closing test PASSED (ledger 2026-10-12 "THE END-STATE CLOSING TEST: PASS";
`designs/endstate/design_endstate_closing_test.md` §6 / §9: Δ̂ +0.596 pp, 90 % [−0.723, +1.915]). The owner's

critic's threat injection, the non-principled op reductions, the coarse KO ramp, the second end-of-turn rule), each
flag with its OFF path, one unit per lane.

**What "delete the OFF path" means here.** After adoption every lever in the `endstate` overlay has ONE value that
anything trains with. Its other value is either a JUDGMENT, an approximate fact, a duplicate rule, an incoherent
reduction, the legacy encoding, or a fact NOT handed to the network. Each unit deletes the flag, its `ModelVersion`
field, its argparse entry and its `_resolve` line, and leaves the adopted behaviour unconditional (or conditional
on the prerequisites it already requires, §0 D-LD2). The JUDGMENT list is `design_hand_computed_features.md`'s rows
marked **J**: O10 `turns_since_progress`, P2 `neutralization` / `tempo_cost` (and P3, which carries the same two
coordinates). Every other row below is the OFF side of an adopted lever, classed in the inventory's "kind" column.

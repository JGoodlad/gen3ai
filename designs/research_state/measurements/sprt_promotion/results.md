# SPRT promotion — operating characteristics

`gen3_sprt_promotion_v1` · H0 0.5 vs H1 0.55 (mean per-game SCORE, a draw = 1/2) · alpha = beta = 0.05 · Wald LLR bounds [-2.944, 2.944] · pentanomial GSPRT over mirrored pairs (Van den Bergh constrained MLE, lambda by bisection) · checked every 40 pairs · 20,000 runs per cell · seed 0 · 5 sentinels round-robin (logit offsets 0.4 x (-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1)) · team-imbalance tau in [0.0, 1.0, 2.0] logits

**Cap: 1680 pairs** (3360 games) — p95 pairs-to-decide at the worst (true rate, tau) cell of the untruncated Wald test, rounded up to a whole batch (p95 = 1680 at rate 0.525, tau 0). Reaching the cap = REJECT.

**Chosen: wald/min40** — min_pairs = smallest grid value whose Wald rule holds both error rates <= nominal everywhere BY THE MONTE-CARLO MARGIN (upper 95% bound); bounds = Wald unless a Siegmund rule holds them by that margin too AND spends <= 85% of the Wald rule's pairs. Siegmund vs the chosen Wald rule, by minimum: min40: errors held by the margin False, pairs saved 21.3%; min80: errors held by the margin False, pairs saved 19.8%; min160: errors held by the margin True, pairs saved 4.5%.

P(promote) at a true rate <= 0.50 is the FALSE-PROMOTION rate (target <= alpha); P(reject) at a true rate >= 0.55 is the FALSE-REJECTION rate (target <= beta). Between them is the indifference zone.

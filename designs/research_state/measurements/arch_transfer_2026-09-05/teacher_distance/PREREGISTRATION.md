# PRE-REGISTRATION — H5, "teacher distance dose-response"

**Frozen 2026-09-05, before any state was generated, any teacher was loaded, and any fold delta
was recomputed.** Nothing below this line is edited after data. Results go in `README.md` under
`## RESULTS`.

## The hypothesis (given, verbatim)

> Across every fold for which the ledger has a banked untaught (off-slice) win-rate delta vs
> parent, the fold's untaught delta **decreases** with its teachers' mean off-slice distance from
> the fold parent, `D_off = mean over the fold's teachers of KL(teacher ‖ parent)` on untaught
> states.

## Why this is being asked

Two landed probes put two points on a line. `content_locality` (H1) found that **locality does not
separate robbing teachers from neutral ones — MAGNITUDE does**: the funded (robbing) teachers sit
farther from the fold parent *everywhere*, untaught-state `KL(teacher‖parent)` **+0.098
[+0.055, +0.149]** above the unfunded ones, while the ratio taught/untaught does not move. And
v8's teachers — the ones whose fold GIFTED — are *local*: little off-slice divergence from their
own parent. This probe asks whether those two points are a line.

## Predictions, scored as written

* **(i) PRIMARY.** Spearman rank correlation between `D_off` and the fold's untaught delta is
  **negative**, and its cluster-bootstrap CI **excludes zero**.
* **(ii)** `D_off` **separates** the folds that ROBBED from those that were NEUTRAL or GIFTED.
* **(iii)** On-slice distance `D_on` (KL on the fold's own taught teams) does **NOT** predict the
  untaught delta once `D_off` is accounted for — the on-slice gain is ~+5pp everywhere.

## The registered NULL

**Fewer than ~6 usable folds, or a CI that includes zero ⇒ NOT DETECTED, never a direction.**

I add one clause of my own, registered here because the fold table is assembled after this file is
frozen and I can already see the shape of the risk: **`D_off` is a property of a TEACHER SET, not
of a fold.** Many folds share one teacher set exactly (the dose arms, B2, C1, rev-4 and COMPFOLD
all distil the identical three R4S3 networks). Ties on the x-axis do not add independent
information about a *slope*, however many folds carry them. So the primary statistic is reported
**twice**: once over folds (the brief's unit) and once over **distinct teacher-set × parent
points** (the honest unit for a dose-response). If the fold-level correlation is significant and
the point-level one is not, the verdict is **NOT DETECTED beyond the pair that carries it**.

## What is measured, and how

`D_off` = mean over the fold's teachers of the mean **forward `KL(teacher ‖ parent)`** over legal
actions, on untaught-team decision states generated by **the fold's own parent piloting** those
teams. `masked_kl_rows` is **imported** from `agents.training.instrumented_ppo.distill_anchor` —
the same formula the live `--distill-anchor-monitor` logs — never reimplemented. `D_on` is the
identical statistic on the fold's own **taught** teams.

The state recipe is copied verbatim from `content_locality/gen_era_locality.py` (itself copied from
`reuse_batch_2026-09-03/offline_collateral_kl/offline_collateral_kl.py`): parent-piloted,
`stochastic=True`, per-player policy seeds `71000/72000 + team_index`, sim seed
`[team_index+1,2,3,4]`, pool sequence `random.Random(61000 + team_index)`, `concurrency=1`, rust
bridge. The untaught 8 hold indices 0..7 so their states reproduce the canonical batch, and that
reproduction is printed as a cross-check.

**Each fold's OWN parent is the reference.** v8's numbers are REUSED from `content_locality`'s
`v8_era_n9.json` rather than regenerated.

## What the answer cannot be

* Not causal. `D_off` is not manipulated here; it is read off teachers that already exist.
* Confounded with teacher training budget by construction on the funded/unfunded axis — the funded
  teachers are the unfunded ones plus 1.0M steps/team. The README must say whether **any** fold in
  the table breaks that confound.
* `D_off` is measured on parent-piloted states, which is not the distribution the fold's own
  rollouts see. These levels are never merged with the live `distill/collateral_kl_vs_parent`.
* Dose and coefficient vary across folds and are reported as covariates, not controlled.

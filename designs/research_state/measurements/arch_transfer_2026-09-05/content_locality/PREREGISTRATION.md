# PRE-REGISTRATION — content locality of a specialist teacher's divergence

Written **2026-09-05, before any state was generated or any KL computed.** Frozen; the README
records the result and quotes this file rather than restating it.

## The claim under test

A specialist teacher's divergence from its fold parent is either

* **LOCAL** — concentrated on the teams the teacher was trained on (team-specific tactics; the
  "leak-shaped" content), or
* **GLOBAL** — of similar magnitude on teams it never saw (general skill; the "gift-shaped"
  content).

Statistic: **forward `KL(teacher ‖ parent)`** over legal actions per decision state, on a state
batch generated by the **parent** piloting each team (so the state distribution is fixed and
identical for every teacher — the only thing that varies is the network being scored).

Locality ratio **`L = KL_taught / KL_untaught`**. `L = 1` ⇒ perfectly global. `L ≫ 1` ⇒ local.

## H1 — the cross-era claim (the brief's primary)

Gen-era teachers (pointer-native head, physics in closed form) are **MORE local** than v8-era
teachers (flat action head, refine loop): `L_gen > L_v8`.

If `L` is similar across eras, or reversed, **the architecture story is dead and this file says so.**

## H2 — the within-era claim (more decisive; no architecture confound)

The ledger's teacher-content 2×2 found **funded** teachers (R5FUND forks, 8 of them) rob the fold's
untaught win rate while **unfunded** teachers (their R5F parents) are parent-neutral. If locality is
what predicts robbery, the funded teachers should be **MORE local**: `L_funded > L_unfunded`.

## My own prediction, registered so it can be wrong

I expect the funded teachers to carry **larger absolute KL on both sets** (they are strictly
further-trained forks of the unfunded ones, so they have travelled further from the shared parent),
and I expect `L_funded ≈ L_unfunded` or **slightly LOWER** — i.e. the extra funded training is
ordinary PPO that moves the whole policy, not extra team-specific tactics. That is the *opposite*
direction to H2. I register it explicitly because the competing mechanistic reading is equally
coherent and the brief's framing favours the other one:

* **H2's reading**: local content = idiosyncratic, over-fitted tactics that transfer badly, so
  locality causes robbery.
* **My reading**: robbery is off-slice displacement, and a teacher can only displace the student
  off-slice if it *itself* differs off-slice — so GLOBAL teachers should rob and LOCAL ones should
  not.

The measurement adjudicates. Whichever way it lands, the direction is reported with its CI and a
CI straddling zero is written **NOT DETECTED**, never a direction.

## Pre-registered analysis

1. **Cluster unit is the TEAM.** Never the state. Per-team means first, then an equal-weight
   bootstrap (20 000 draws) over the cluster set, with **one fixed resampling index set shared by
   every teacher**, so teacher-vs-teacher differences are paired on the same team draws.
2. **Primary A (the brief's form).** Per teacher: `KL_taught` = mean over its own 2 taught teams,
   `KL_untaught` = mean over the 8 untaught teams, `L` = the ratio. Group-level `L` pooled over the
   8 teachers of a half, bootstrap over **teachers** (8 clusters).
3. **Primary B (sibling control, better powered).** Every one of the 16 taught teams was taught by
   exactly one teacher of each half. For team `t`, contrast that teacher's KL against the mean KL
   of the **7 sibling teachers of the same half** on the *same* team and the *same* states.
   `R(t) = KL_own(t) / KL_siblings(t)`, bootstrap over the **16 teams**. This holds the state
   distribution, the team, and the training recipe fixed; only "did this teacher train on this
   team" varies. Registered as the decisive within-era read.
4. **MATCHED-NOISE FLOOR (mandatory).** The same statistic between two nearby same-run checkpoints
   of the parent, on the same states. Any teacher whose KL on one side is inside that floor has
   nothing to measure there and is reported **WITHIN FLOOR**, not as a small effect.
5. **Verdict vocabulary**: SIGNIFICANT / WITHIN FLOOR / NOT DETECTED.

# PREDICTION — THE STEP CURVE

**Registered 2026-09-11, BEFORE any cycle was generated.** Nothing in this file was written
after a number existed. The reads it commits to are run afterwards, unchanged.

## The question

The critic ladder ran ten 10M arms — ten different levers on the win-prob critic — and returned
**zero detected registered rows**. The N-curve (ledger 2026-09-10 *THE N-CURVE*) then showed the
conditioning HEAD saturates within ~4,000 battles **for a fixed representation**: more eval buys
no more conditioning once the representation is fixed.

That leaves one axis nobody on the ladder varied, because every arm was 10M:

> **Does the REPRESENTATION keep improving with training STEPS beyond 10M, at a fixed
> configuration?**

If it does, the ladder's null is a statement about 10M, not about the critic — and the cheapest
open lever is *more steps*, not another architectural arm. If it does not, 10M is where this
configuration's opponent conditioning lands and the ladder's null is the critic's null.

## The instrument

`models/ai_v12_02_winprob_critic` — the completed 75M run, same argv lineage as the ladder's
control (three tokens differ: `--pin-commit`, `--run-name`, `--steps`; see README §2 for the pin
diff and the flag whose DEFAULT moved between them). Four checkpoints:

| label | step |
|---|---|
| 10M | 9,969,408 |
| 20M | 18,660,864 |
| 40M | 40,935,168 |
| 73M | 73,121,280 |

**Two independent offline eval draws per checkpoint** (seeds 20260909 and 20260910), 400 games ×
12 opponents, full capture, `--concurrency 1`, generated by `main.ops.eval_trace_gen`. Eight
cycles. Each checkpoint is read against the run's OWN 10M checkpoint — the WITHIN-RUN curve, which
is the point — draw-matched (draw1 vs draw1, draw2 vs draw2).

## The decision row

**`cond.opp_class_auc.t4_10`** — the turns-4–10 opponent-class AUC of `V`. It is the decision row
for three reasons banked before this measurement existed:

1. It is the **only conditioning row on the campaign with a two-draw floor smaller than plausible
   effects** — 0.0219 at hp800 (ledger 2026-09-11 *MEASUREMENT ×2* (B)).
2. Turn 1 is **Bayes-optimal at 0.50** on a matched-team frame (Gen 3 has no team preview), and
   the window where the question has an answer is turns 4–10 — `value_pooled` → opponent class
   reads 0.50 / 0.67 / **0.82** at t1 / t1–3 / t4–10.
3. The whole 10M roster sits in a 0.026-wide band on it: **0.688 / 0.705 / 0.707 / 0.709 / 0.714**
   (`ctrl10M_c` / `lambda09` / `ctrl10M_b` / `ctrl10M` / `denseaux`), reproducing the N-curve's
   independent 0.72 / 0.70. Ten levers moved it by less than the floor. If seven-fold more
   training steps also move it by less than the floor, the row is not lever-limited and not
   step-limited — it is representation-limited at something the campaign has not touched.

Secondary registered rows: `gate.resolution.bot`, `gate.resolution.all`, `cond.opp_class_auc.t1_3`.
Reported-not-decided: `cond.spread_ratio.t1_3` / `.t4_10` (run-level floors 0.19–0.22 — undecidable
at one run), `cond.own_team_r2.t1` (**PROVISIONAL by rule 18** — it needs ≥2 offline draws per
checkpoint, which this design finally supplies, but its floor is a control-vs-control quantity this
single run cannot price), identity bias ALL/late (rule 19 run-level), ECE, calibration slope.

## THE BAR — registered before the data

A rise counts **only if the between-checkpoint delta exceeds the within-checkpoint two-draw spread
at every step of the rise.** Concretely, writing `A(s, d)` for the decision row at step `s`, draw
`d`, and `W(s) = |A(s,1) − A(s,2)|` for that checkpoint's own eval-draw spread:

- **(i) "the representation keeps improving with steps" is SUPPORTED** iff, for the consecutive
  pairs 10M→20M→40M→73M, every step is **UP** and each `A(s') − A(s)` **exceeds
  `max(W(s), W(s'))`** on both draws, giving a monotone rise of at least
  `3 × max W` end to end. The 10M→73M delta must additionally carry a battle-clustered CI
  clear of zero on both draws.
- **(ii) "flat" is SUPPORTED** iff the 10M→73M delta is **within `max(W)` across all four
  checkpoints** and its CI covers zero on both draws — i.e. seven-fold more steps buys less than
  one re-draw of the same checkpoint.
- **Anything else is UNDECIDED** and is reported as such: a non-monotone curve, a rise smaller
  than the draw spread, a rise on one draw and not the other. **Non-monotone REFUTES a smooth
  step-response; it is not explained away.**

This is rule 19 applied in advance: the eval-draw component is the one that dominates these rows
and the one fixed by RE-DRAWING a checkpoint, which is exactly what the second seed buys. Four
single draws would give four points and no width; four pairs give four points each with its own
width, and a curve is a claim about the gaps between points relative to that width.

**Magnitude anchor.** `W` has measured ±0.02–0.03 on the decoder rows, and the hp800 two-draw floor
on this row is 0.0219 — at 400 games per draw the eval-draw component is WIDER than at 800, so
0.022 is a **lower** bound on what `W` will be here and the bar cannot be softened by borrowing it.
The bar is `W` as measured on these eight cycles, not a borrowed floor.

## What each hypothesis predicts on the decision row

| | (i) representation improves with steps | (ii) flat |
|---|---|---|
| `cond.opp_class_auc.t4_10` @10M | ≈ the 10M roster band, 0.69–0.71 | same |
| @73M | **> 0.75**, i.e. ≥ 3 draw-spreads above 10M and outside the roster's entire 0.026 band | 0.69–0.71, inside the roster band |
| monotonicity | up at all three consecutive steps, each gap > `max W` | sign flips freely between steps; gaps < `W` |
| `gate.resolution.bot` | up with steps (a sharper critic on the fixed bot panel) | inside the draw spread |
| what it means for the ladder | the 10M null is a statement about 10M; **steps are an untested lever and the cheapest one open** | the 10M null is the critic's null; conditioning is capped by something neither levers nor steps reach |

**My prior, stated so it can be wrong.** I expect **(ii) or UNDECIDED-leaning-flat** on the class
AUC, and a **modest real rise on `gate.resolution.bot`**. Reasons: the N-curve showed the head
saturating against a fixed representation; the ladder's ten levers moved the row by less than the
floor; and the run's own strength keeps climbing over 10M→73M while the conditioning question is
about *what the value head encodes about WHO it is playing*, not about how well it plays. A clean
(i) would genuinely surprise me, and is the more valuable outcome — it would reopen the campaign on
an axis that costs GPU rather than ideas.

## Confounds named in advance (quantified in the README, not discovered there)

1. **SENTINEL STRENGTH DRIFT — the big one.** The sentinels are the run's OWN pool snapshots below
   the read step, so a later checkpoint faces STRONGER sentinels. Three of the twelve opponent
   cells therefore change with the x-axis. **The nine scripted bots are FIXED and are the same
   nine at every checkpoint**, so a bot-stratum row (`gate.resolution.bot`) is clean and a
   12-cell row (`cond.opp_class_auc.*`) is not. Both directions are arguable — stronger sentinels
   are harder to separate from each other (pushing the AUC DOWN with steps, biasing against (i)),
   and they are also further from the bots (pushing it UP). The confound is named on every pool
   row; the bot rows carry the clean reading. The sentinel steps each checkpoint resolved to are
   recorded in the README table.
2. **The pin and the REGIME.** The 75M run is at `f971caf2` (Sep 6), the ladder control at
   `f3502568` (Sep 8). They straddle the 2026-09-07 opponent-regime boundary:
   `--eval-sentinel-greedy` **defaulted False** at the older pin and **True** at the newer, and
   the 75M run's `model_config.json` (config_version 110) records no regime at all. Its cycles are
   therefore generated under the PRE-boundary asymmetric regime — the regime it was actually
   evaluated in — and `critic_read` **refuses** a delta against the post-boundary ladder controls.
   That refusal is correct and expected. **The within-run curve is unaffected**: all eight cycles
   share one regime, one run, one spec. The control read is attempted and its refusal recorded;
   any cross-run comparison is reported as LEVELS side by side, never as a delta.
3. **ONE RUN.** Every point is one training trajectory. A step curve from one run cannot separate
   "steps" from "this run's path". Rule 19: a run-level row needs run replicates, which this does
   not have. The decision row is eval-draw-level and gets its width from the re-draws; the
   run-level rows (identity bias, the spread ratios, the calibration slope) are REPORTED only and
   no verdict rests on them.
4. **EVAL-vs-TRAINING POPULATION.** A fixed 12-opponent panel is not the training mix (ledger
   2026-09-11 *MEASUREMENT ×2* (A) — that gap survived the team-assignment finding untouched).
5. **γ.** `eval_trace_gen` reads gamma from `model_config.json`, which records none for either
   run, so every offline cycle in this campaign — mine and the landed ladder's — is generated at
   γ = 0.99 while both runs trained at γ = 1.0. It reaches only `BattleRecorder._td_residuals`,
   which no row in `main.ops` consumes, and it is constant across all eight of my cycles. Reported,
   not corrected, and not load-bearing.

## What will NOT be claimed

- No cross-run delta against `ai_v12_11_ladder_ctrl10M` or any ladder arm (confound 2).
- No claim on a row whose floor this design cannot price (`cond.own_team_r2.t1` is PROVISIONAL;
  the spread ratios and the calibration slope are REPORTED).
- No verdict from a single draw, and no verdict from a rise smaller than the draw spread.
- No strength claim. ELO is not read here.

## AMENDMENT — the sentinel panel, 2026-09-11

**Written while the FIRST cycle was still playing and before any cycle was read.** It is recorded
here rather than silently applied, because it changes a named confound and the bar must be fixed
before the numbers are.

**What forced it.** The task specified `--sentinels 3`, which draws the run's own pool snapshots
*below* the read step. The 75M run's retained pool starts at **36,000,000** — its bounded
self-play pool evicted everything earlier during training — so:

- at **10M** and **20M** there is NO snapshot below the read step, and `eval_trace_gen` REFUSES
  ("no pool snapshot below step 9969408 to use as a sentinel");
- at **40M** only 36M / 38M / 40M are eligible;
- at **73M** all 23 are.

The specified command therefore cannot produce the curve at all. Worse, if it could, the opponent
panel would be a *different set of policies at every x*, and `cond.opp_class_auc.*` is the
**pool-vs-bot** class AUC — a row whose very definition names the pool cells. A bots-only cycle
(`--sentinels 0`) is not a fallback either: with no pool cells the class has one level and the
decision row does not exist.

**What is done instead.** `--include-current-snapshot --sentinels 3`, which draws three snapshots
spread across the run's whole retained range. Verified before launch: this selects the **identical
panel at all four checkpoints** —

| checkpoint | sentinel_0 | sentinel_1 | sentinel_2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10M / 20M / 40M / 73M | 36,000,000 | 56,000,016 | 74,000,016 |

— with identical opponent labels, so `spec_of` matches across the curve and the four cycles are
four draws of one population in everything but the checkpoint under test.

**What this does to the confounds, in both directions.**

1. **Sentinel-strength drift is ELIMINATED, not merely named.** Confound 1 above said three of the
   twelve cells change with the x-axis. They no longer do: every checkpoint faces the same three
   policies and the same nine bots. This is a strictly better frame than the one registered.
2. **A NEW confound replaces it, and it is CONSERVATIVE for (i).** The panel is fixed at
   *late-run* strength, so the 10M checkpoint faces three opponents far stronger than itself while
   the 73M checkpoint faces near-peers. The pool cells' outcome base rate therefore rises with the
   x-axis while the bot cells' does not, and two classes whose outcomes converge are *harder* to
   separate. The expected direction of this bias on `cond.opp_class_auc.t4_10` is **DOWNWARD with
   steps** — i.e. against hypothesis (i). **A rise observed anyway is therefore stronger evidence
   than the registered design would have given; a fall is confounded and will be reported as
   UNDECIDED on that row, never as evidence for (ii).** That asymmetry is registered here, before
   the data.
3. **The clean row is unchanged.** The nine scripted bots are fixed and identical at every
   checkpoint under either design, so `gate.resolution.bot` carries a reading that no sentinel
   choice touches. Where the pool-bearing row and the bot row disagree, the bot row is the one
   without this confound and is quoted as such.
4. **One sentinel (74,000,016) sits marginally ABOVE the 73M read step.** It is a later self, not
   the same weights — not a self-mirror — but `--include-current-snapshot` is what permits it, and
   it is named here rather than discovered in the report.

**The bar is unchanged.** A rise counts only if every consecutive gap exceeds the larger of the two
checkpoints' own two-draw spreads, on both draws. Adding a confound that pushes the decision row
DOWN does not license a smaller rise to count.

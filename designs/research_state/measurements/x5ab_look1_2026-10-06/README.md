## What was played

`designs/training/eval_and_rating.md`, schedule seed 0, batches of 500 pairs, GPU (T2 graph, 64 envs, RTX 3080 Ti),
one commit (`bcb0296c`) and one engine build for every cell. Rows: the archive's eval COUNT ledger
(`models/_ledger`), purpose `ab`, one family per read and protocol:

| (d) oracle, both-sided | `x5ab_oracle_both_sided` / `x5ab_look1_oracle_both` | same | `..._oracle_both_sided` |

Checkpoints (read-only, resolved explicitly; sha256 in `result.json`): `final_model.zip` of
`rb_x5ab_{blob,fm}_s100{1,2,3}`, `rb_x5ab_oracle_{sp,full}_s1001b / _s1002 / _s1003`; fixed_mass at matched

the 12M checkpoint). The `rb_x5ab_oracle_*_s1001` INCONCLUSIVE artifacts were not played.

**Wall time.** 07:23 → 08:43 PDT, 80 min for the whole look on the GPU: 10 engines (one per request × oracle level ×
opponent arm), each 4–6.5 min of T2 graph compile, then ~9 s per 1,000-game batch (90–127 games/s). Ledger audit
after: 1,272 rows, 0 live claims, OK; 0 aborted games in any cell.

## Step 1: the GPU identity checks (`gpu_identity/`)

`run.sh` + `run_oracle.sh` played five small cells (50 pairs, schedule seed 911, scratch ledgers outside `models/`)
on the CPU (T2 eager) and the GPU (T2 graph): play-many with a same-architecture blob cell on a two-group engine,
the two-architecture cross (fixed_mass × blob), one-sided oracle-species × blob, one-sided oracle-full ×
fixed_mass, and both-sided oracle-species × fixed_mass. `compare.py` → `compare.json`: **all 5 cells EQUAL** —
W/L/D, pentanomial, per-team counters, seed block and the ALL-games outcome digest (every game's result and
length). The clean-games digest differs on one cell only because the two devices' 2e-3 near-tie LISTS differ (game
90 sits inside the bar on the CPU only), so it hashes different game sets; the all-games digest covers that game
and agrees. The first pass put oracle-species and oracle-full into ONE engine, which the pre-flight refused
(`oracle_reveal` is an architecture toggle), so `run_oracle.sh` split them; the driver plays one engine per oracle
level × opponent arm for the same reason.

## The kill / resume test (`killtest.log`)

`play_look1.sh` (STEPS=a, 40 pairs in batches of 20, CPU, a scratch ledger) was SIGKILLed by process group after 3
rows (mid-batch) and restarted: it reported "3 already recorded", replayed the killed batch on its seed block
(its claim voided as a dead writer's) and finished: 18 rows, 19 claims, audit OK.
